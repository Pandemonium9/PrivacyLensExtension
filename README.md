# PrivacyLens

**Instant plain-language privacy policy summaries.**

## Stop agreeing to things you haven't read.

Privacy policies are written by lawyers, for lawyers. The average policy runs **4,000 words** — longer than most short stories — and is deliberately dense enough that most people click **"I Agree"** without reading a single sentence. **PrivacyLens changes that.**

## What PrivacyLens Does

PrivacyLens automatically detects privacy policies on any webpage you visit and offers to analyze them instantly. **One click**, and our AI breaks down exactly what a company is doing with your data — in plain English, in seconds.

No more legalese. No more guessing. Just clear, honest answers about what you're agreeing to.

## What You Get

- 🔍 **Instant detection** — PrivacyLens quietly watches for privacy policy links as you browse. When it finds one, a small, unobtrusive prompt appears asking if you'd like to analyze it. No interruptions unless you want them.
- 💯 **Understandable grading** — Get a comprehensive letter grade showing you how trustworthy the privacy policy seems, just like in school! Assess the risk of giving your information to a site in just one glance.
- ✅ **Green flags** — Things the policy gets right. Companies that limit data collection, don't sell your information, offer easy deletion, or give you meaningful control over your data.
- 🚩 **Red flags** — The things companies hope you won't notice. Data sold to third parties, indefinite retention periods, vague language around law enforcement sharing, or the right to change terms without notice.
- 📊 **Confidence rating** — Not all policies are created equal. Some are clear and specific; others are deliberately vague. PrivacyLens tells you how confident it is in its analysis so you always know how much to trust the summary.
- 📋 **Plain-English summary** — A concise overview of the policy's key points written the way a friend who happens to be a lawyer would explain it to you — honest, direct, and jargon-free.
- 💾 **Smart caching** — Already analyzed a site? PrivacyLens remembers. If the policy hasn't changed since your last visit, you get the cached result instantly with no extra API call. If it has changed, PrivacyLens automatically re-analyzes and flags that the policy was updated.

## Why It Matters

The average person encounters dozens of privacy policies every year. Studies consistently show that reading every policy you encounter would take **hundreds of hours annually** — time nobody actually has. So people click agree. Every time. For everything.

> That's not informed consent. It's learned helplessness.

PrivacyLens doesn't ask you to read everything. It reads it for you, flags what matters, and lets you make an actual decision in **under a minute**. Whether you're signing up for a new service, shopping online, or just curious about what a site knows about you, PrivacyLens gives you the information you need to decide whether you're comfortable proceeding.

## How It Works

When you click **Analyze** on a detected privacy policy, PrivacyLens extracts the policy text from the page and sends it to our secure backend for analysis. Our AI, powered by **Google's Gemini**, reads the full policy and returns a structured breakdown covering:

- Data collection practices
- Third-party sharing
- Retention policies
- User rights
- And more

The result is stored against the site's domain so future visits are instant.

Your analysis history is tied to **domains only**: no account required, no personal profile, and no tracking of which sites you visit beyond what you explicitly choose to analyze.

## Privacy by Design

We built a privacy tool, so we took privacy seriously in how we built it:

- **No account required** — ever
- **No browsing history** collected
- **Only analyzes pages you explicitly choose**
- **Domain names and policy text only** — nothing else leaves your browser
- **Summaries cached by domain**, not by user
- **No advertising, no data brokering, no third-party analytics**

We don't make money by selling what we know about you. We couldn't — **we don't know anything about you.**

## Permissions Explained

PrivacyLens requests only the permissions it genuinely needs:

| Permission | Why we need it |
|---|---|
| **Read the active tab** | To detect privacy policy links on the current page and extract policy text when you click Analyze |
| **Storage** | To cache analysis results locally so repeat visits are instant |
| **Scripting** | To scan page content for privacy policy links |

We **do not** request access to your browsing history, bookmarks, passwords, or any other sensitive browser data.

## Who Is This For?

PrivacyLens is for anyone who uses the internet, which is everyone. But it's especially useful if you:

- Sign up for new services regularly and want to know what you're agreeing to
- Work in a role where data privacy matters (legal, compliance, security, journalism)
- Are a parent helping a younger family member understand what apps and sites collect
- Simply care about where your personal data ends up and want a fast, reliable way to check
